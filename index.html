GigBid: a multi-agent freelancer copilot

LangGraph agents watch a freelance marketplace, decide which jobs are worth a bid, write proposals in which every claim cites the freelancer's own portfolio, catch scams and injected instructions, and stop before anything is sent: a human approves every proposal and every message. AI-4016 R&D assignment by Syed Kalbe Raza (22i-0794), FAST-NUCES Islamabad.

3-minute demo

2 min 28 s, captioned (no voice-over). Recorded on the full local stack against GigSim, the project's marketplace simulator, with a synthetic full-stack persona. The two minutes in which the agents analyse the job and write the draft are shown at 12× speed; everything else is real time. Download MP4
