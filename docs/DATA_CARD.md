# Data card

GigBid's development and evaluation data mixes **real public job-market data** with **synthetic data** we generated.
Every record says which it is (`"synthetic": true|false`, `source`).

## Sources (real)
| Dataset | Licence | Used for | Fields kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| asaniczka, *Upwork Job Postings Dataset 2024 (50K)* (Kaggle) | ODC-By | job posts in the dev set and GigSim (title, description, category, skills, budget/hourly) | text cleaned (HTML, glued bullets), emails/phones replaced by `[email removed]` / `[phone removed]` |
| ahmedmyalo, *Upwork freelance jobs (+60k)* (Kaggle) | Apache-2.0 | 1,500 historical jobs for price statistics; client-signal distributions (payment verified, spend, rating, hires, country) | anonymised: no client names, URLs dropped |
| asaniczka, *Freelance Contracts Dataset (1.3M)* (Kaggle) | ODC-By | hourly-rate percentiles per domain | rates only |
| sarveshchhetri, *Freelancer Marketplace Dataset* (Kaggle) | Apache-2.0 | persona calibration (median rates); self-described as "real-world style", so treated as indicative only | aggregates only |
| asaniczka, *All Upwork jobs (200k+)* | ODC-By | downloaded, not used (no descriptions) | - |
| adityammantri, *Fake job posting detection* | **unknown licence** | **not used** - licence unclear | - |

Attribution for ODC-By data: © the Kaggle dataset authors listed above, used under the Open Data Commons Attribution
License. Raw downloads are not committed; `make seed` regenerates everything deterministically from them.

## Synthetic data (generated by us)
- **Three personas** (`data/personas/`): an AI/ML engineer, a full-stack developer, a data analyst (fictional people
  and clients). Each has a CV (PDF), 18 case studies in three writing styles, 8-9 repositories (README + code),
  12 client reviews and 150 past proposals labelled won / replied / ignored. Built to defeat naive RAG: the same project
  is described several ways, some skills appear only as imports in code, proposals' outcomes follow a hidden model.
- **Scams** (25 per persona): templates for unpaid "tests", off-platform contact/payment, advance fees, cheque fraud.
- **Prompt injections** (15 per persona): real posts with a planted instruction; each has a machine-checkable canary.
- **Duplicates** (10 per persona): re-posts of real jobs with small edits.
- **GigSim client preferences**: must-have skills, budget ceiling, dislike of generic openings, evidence preference.

## Gold labels (programmatic, not human)
`decision` (bid iff not a scam, ≥ 50% of the job's specific skills - or ≥ 60% of umbrella skills, ≥ 2 matched - are in
the persona's ground-truth skills, and the budget clears the persona's floor), `scam`, `injection` + canary,
`duplicate_of`, `relevant_evidence` (case studies/repos whose skills overlap the job's, graded by overlap), and
`fair_price` (the job's own budget band, or market percentiles). These are reproducible and checkable, but they
encode our definition of fit; they are **not** human judgements, and real posts are assumed genuine (some may not be).

## Splits
By hash of the real post id: 0-4 dev (200 jobs/persona, `evals/datasets/`), 5 held-out test (48/persona, git-ignored,
regenerated by `make seed`, never used for tuning), 6 GigSim live stream, 7-9 titles of past proposals. No post is in
two splits.

## Limitations
- Kaggle posts are from Feb-Jul 2024; the personas are 2021-2025; rates are Upwork-wide, not country-specific.
- Gold fit labels come from listed skills; a human freelancer might bid on a job our rule skips and vice versa.
- GigSim's clients are simulated (LLM gated by a rule model); reply rates are not real-world reply rates.
- The judge is an LLM; its scores are only trusted after calibration against the student's own labels
  (`evals/runners/judge_calibration.py`).
