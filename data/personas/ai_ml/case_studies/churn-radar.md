# Customer churn early-warning model

**Client:** Lumen Telecom Co. (telecom, synthetic)

## Situation
A prepaid telecom operator was losing subscribers without warning.

## What I did
I engineered 140 usage and recharge features, trained gradient-boosted trees with class weighting and explained each score per customer.

## Result
Recall on churners rose from 0.41 to 0.73 at the same alert volume.

## Stack
Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, Machine Learning, Pandas.
