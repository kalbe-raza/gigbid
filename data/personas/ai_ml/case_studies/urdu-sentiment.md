# Urdu and English review sentiment classifier (2022)

- **Challenge:** an online marketplace could not read the mixed Urdu/English reviews it received.
- **Solution:** fine-tuned a multilingual transformer on 18k hand-checked reviews with code-switching augmentation.
- **Result:** macro-F1 of 0.86 on a held-out month.
- **Client sector:** e-commerce.
