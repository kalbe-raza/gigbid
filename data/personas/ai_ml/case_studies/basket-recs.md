# Product recommendation engine

**Client:** Harbor E-Commerce Co. (e-commerce, synthetic)

## Situation
A home-goods store showed the same bestsellers to everyone.

## What I did
I trained implicit-feedback matrix factorisation and served precomputed neighbours from a cache.

## Result
Add-to-cart rate up 9% in a two-week A/B test.
