# Store-level demand forecasting

**Client:** Bluegate Retail Co. (retail, synthetic)

## Situation
A 60-store grocery chain over-ordered perishables every week.

## What I did
I combined holiday-aware baselines with a global gradient-boosting model per SKU and scheduled nightly retraining.

## Result
Forecast MAPE fell from 18% to 11% on fresh produce.

## Stack
Time Series Forecasting, Python, Prophet, Pandas.
