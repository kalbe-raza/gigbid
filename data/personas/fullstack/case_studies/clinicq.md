# Clinic appointment booking platform

**Client:** Northwind Healthcare Co. (healthcare, synthetic)

## Situation
A chain of dental clinics took bookings by phone and lost a quarter of them to no-shows.

## What I did
I built online booking with reminders, waitlists and a front-desk view.

## Result
No-shows fell by 27% in three months.
